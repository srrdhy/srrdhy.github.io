FF-PINN Fourier Feature Embedding

Created2025-06-17|Updated2026-11-04|PINN
|Post Views:

FF-PINN means adding a Fourier feature embedding to the input. By mapping the PINN’s input coordinates into a higher-dimensional space with sine functions, this method strengthens the model’s detail representation and thereby improves its ability to capture fine-scale detail.

Specifically, the following transformation is performed:
$$ \gamma_{i}(X) = \begin{bmatrix} \cos(2\pi \beta_{i} X) \\ \sin(2\pi \beta_{i} X) \end{bmatrix}, \quad \text{for } i=1, 2, \ldots, S $$
Here $\gamma$ denotes the FF, $X$ denotes the input vector of the PINN, and the parameter $\beta$ is drawn from a Gaussian distribution $N(\mu, \sigma)$.
