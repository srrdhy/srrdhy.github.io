1. Implementing the Discrete Fourier Transform in Python

While working with recorded videos, I noticed background noise. How do we remove it from the audio?

We need to identify the frequency of the noise and then filter it out.

How do we determine the frequency? The answer is the Fourier frequency.

Decompose the signal into sine waves and cosine waves; we can then identify the amplitude, frequency and phase of each wave, and by plotting each frequency we can isolate the amplitudes that contribute most to the signal.

The Fourier transform of a continuous function:
$$\hat{f}(k)=\int_{-\infty}^\infty f(t)e^{-i2\pi kt}dt$$
But in reality, signals are discrete, so we have the discrete Fourier transform (DFT):
$$X[k]=\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}x[n]e^{-i2\pi\frac{k}{N}n}$$
Here:

  • $N$ is the number of samples
  • $n$ is the index of the current sample
  • $k$ is the current frequency
  • $x[n]$ is the signal value at $n$
  • $X[k]$ is the DFT corresponding to the current $k$
